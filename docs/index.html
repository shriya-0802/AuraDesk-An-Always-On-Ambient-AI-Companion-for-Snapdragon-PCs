Privacy Guardian

Designed for enterprise security in public spaces. The AI accurately counts faces in real-time. If shoulder surfing is detected, it instantly overlays a massive privacy screen warning to protect your sensitive data.

🧠

Autonomous Wellness (AWA)

Acting as an "Executive Function" assistant for neurodivergent users (ADHD/focus disorders), AuraDesk tracks sustained stress and fatigue. It autonomously intervenes by enabling Do Not Disturb and toggling Dark Mode to reduce cognitive load.

🖐️

Motor-Accessible Gestures

Control your entire OS hands-free. Perfect for users with physical motor impairments. Use a fist to take screenshots, an open palm to control media, or swipe gestures to seamlessly switch virtual desktops.

Standalone Edge Deployment

Traditional AI apps rely on the cloud, introducing massive privacy risks for a webcam app. AuraDesk is different.

  • Zero Cloud Dependencies: Everything runs locally on your PC.
  • Optimized for Edge: Designed to run on the Snapdragon NPU.
  • One-Click Native App: Using our custom build script, the entire architecture compiles into a single standalone `.exe` (Windows) or `.app` (macOS).
